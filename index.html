A few things to know

WALWAL streams videos through the official YouTube player. Lyrics are displayed by the video creator. An internet connection is required; ads and regional or embed restrictions may apply.

Starting tracks are from Atomic Karaoke. Original artists are credited in the songbook. Videos, audio, and lyrics are not downloaded or hosted by WALWAL.

Favorites, added songs, history, and your queue are saved only in this browser. YouTube receives requests when you play a video. Microphone audio is processed locally, with no recording, scoring, or upload.
